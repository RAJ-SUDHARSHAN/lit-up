Quick Rules

  • Play with 4–10 players in two teams.
  • Ask an opponent for a card — you must hold one from the same set.
  • Got it? Keep your turn. Missed? Turn passes to them.
  • Declare a set when your team holds all 6 cards.
  • Wrong declaration? The other team scores.
  • First team to 5 sets wins.
The 9 sets

Each suit: Low (2–7) and High (9–A). Plus one wild set: all four 8s and both Jokers.
